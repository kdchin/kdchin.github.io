I am pursuing my PhD in Computer Science at The University of British Columbia, advised by Dr. Reid Holmes. I hope to one day catch it. My research sits at the intersection of humans in software engineering and computer science education. I an interested in studying how tools, abstractions, and collaboration support continual learning in software development.

Before my PhD, I was a software engineer at a health-AI startup (before it was cool (or not cool)) and completed a double major at Carnegie Mellon University in information systems and computer science.

Research Interests

Program comprehension · software engineering education · human factors in software development · AI-assisted programming · empirical software engineering
